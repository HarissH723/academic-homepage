Undergraduate researcher in the Zhongrui Wang Lab at SUSTech (July 2024–present). My work explores memristive devices, analog in-memory computing, and device-aware learning methods for efficient AI hardware.

Current interests include using non-ideal device behavior as a computing resource and co-designing devices, circuits, and algorithms for neuromorphic systems.
