**Team project · July 2025**

I led a three-person team designing a five-stage pipelined RV32I processor in Verilog. The design included control and hazard handling, a two-bit saturating-counter branch predictor, and a four-way set-associative cache.

We verified the processor on a Xilinx Nexys A7 FPGA at 185 MHz. The project connected architecture decisions to timing, verification, and hardware implementation.
