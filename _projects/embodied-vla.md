**Team project · March–May 2026**

I led a four-person team investigating residual reinforcement learning for vision-language-action manipulation and conducted parameter-efficient fine-tuning of the 3-billion-parameter GR00T model.

Our workflows included SO101 with LeRobot and Isaac Sim for data preparation, training, and evaluation. On the Unitree G1-D platform, I worked on data collection and conversion, SmolVLA inference, and deployment support. The emphasis was on connecting model adaptation with practical robotic execution and validation.
