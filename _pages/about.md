## Introduction

I am an undergraduate student in Microelectronics at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), expecting to graduate in July 2027. My research interests lie at the intersection of emerging memory devices, in-memory and neuromorphic computing, and efficient AI hardware.

I am particularly interested in **device–algorithm co-design**: understanding how device variability, stochasticity, temporal dynamics, and programming constraints can be incorporated into computing and learning systems, rather than treated only as hardware non-idealities.

<p class="qs-arc">Device Physics → Computing Primitive → Learning Algorithm → Hardware System</p>

## Selected Research

- **Adaptive resistive-memory programming for continual learning** — Python simulation of device variability in memristor arrays, robustness-testing workflows, and representation analysis (PCA, t-SNE, confusion matrices) for class-incremental learning. Zhongrui Wang Lab, SUSTech, July 2024–present. [Read more]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }})
- **Ta/Al₂O₃ device fabrication and characterization** — cleanroom process flow and electrical/failure analysis, March–May 2026. [Read more]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }})
- **Hardware and system experience** — a five-stage RV32I processor verified at 185 MHz on a Xilinx Nexys A7 FPGA, and vision-language-action workflows on SO-101 and Unitree G1-D platforms. [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})
