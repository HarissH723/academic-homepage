I am **Qunsheng Hou**, an undergraduate researcher in Microelectronics at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). My work connects **memristive devices**, **in-memory computing**, **device-aware learning**, and **efficient AI hardware**. I plan to pursue a PhD beginning in 2027.

In the Zhongrui Wang Lab, I study how the behavior and variability of resistive memory can inform robust, efficient learning. I am particularly interested in translating device-level constraints into practical computing systems.

## Research focus

Memristive devices → in-memory and neuromorphic computing → device-aware learning → efficient AI hardware.
